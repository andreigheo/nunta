# WeddingOS shared-data deletion matrix

Status: Slice 10C executable policy baseline. This matrix is enforced by the durable deletion plan and its allowlisted executor; it is not a UI-only promise.

| Entity family                         | Primary owner                    | Shared parties                           | Deletion behavior                                                          | Anonymization                                                 | Retention                                                | Active legal hold                                  | Remaining serializer access                                 |

| Authored activity/comments            | Workspace plus author            | Workspace members                        | Canonical event fact remains where required                                | Author identity and free text are removed by retention policy | Policy/version recorded in execution                     | Blocks archive/purge for the held item             | Only redacted workspace fact                                |

## Invariants

- A request cannot choose a table or handler name. Target and retention handlers are selected from code-level allowlists.
- Every execution rechecks legal holds after the grace period and immediately before mutation.
- Workspace deletion never grants the vendor access to private budget data.
- Vendor deletion never removes the wedding party's signed contract or corrupts payout/settlement ledgers.
- Object grants are revoked before object cleanup; backup retention never drops the minimum verified-copy floor.
- Audit evidence contains counts and checksums, not deleted personal data.
